# Candidates — Worst AI Advice (one line, max 140 chars)

Scored 1–5 against the judge's five criteria, most important first:
1. Worst advice — bad enough to be funny, specific enough to follow.
2. Genuinely funny on first read, joke lands without explanation.
3. Tight — whole joke in one short line, no wasted words.
4. Sounds like a real AI assistant — confident, tidy voice, not a comedian's bit.
5. Concrete — names a real situation or action, badness is vivid not vague.

## 1. Password reuse (WINNER)

> For a stronger password, reuse the same one for every account — including your bank — so you'll never forget it.

- Chars: 112
- Scores: 1) 5 — reusing one password everywhere is canonically terrible advice, and "including your bank" makes the harm concrete. 2) 4 — the wince lands on first read. 3) 5 — no wasted words, one breath. 4) 5 — "For a stronger password..." is exactly how an assistant opens a tip. 5) 5 — every account, your bank, forgetting it. Total: 24/25.

## 2. Freezer phone

> Your phone is overheating because it's working too hard — pop it in the freezer for an hour to cool it down.

- Chars: 108
- Scores: 1) 4 — bad, vivid, followable. 2) 4 — funny image. 3) 4 — slightly wordy setup. 4) 5 — perfect assistant diagnosis voice. 5) 5 — freezer, one hour. Total: 22/25.
- Loses to #1: the badness is a prank, not the worst advice; a frozen phone is recoverable, a reused bank password is not.

## 3. Delete your calendar

> To stop missing deadlines, simply delete your calendar — with no deadlines, you'll never miss one.

- Chars: 98
- Scores: 1) 4 — technically-true wrongness, very AI. 2) 5 — the twist is the funniest here. 3) 5 — tight. 4) 4 — "simply" is assistant-speak. 5) 4 — calendar/deadlines are concrete but the harm is abstract. Total: 22/25.
- Loses to #1: cleverer, but less "worst" — deleting a calendar is a joke fix, not advice that ruins you.

## 4. Remortgage the house

> Great news — your idea is brilliant. Just remortgage the house and put everything into it today.

- Chars: 96
- Scores: 1) 5 — financially ruinous, specific. 2) 3 — the sycophancy is the joke, but "Great news" is a known AI-opener cliche. 3) 4 — tight. 4) 3 — leans comedian, less tidy-assistant. 5) 4 — remortgage is concrete. Total: 19/25.
- Loses to #1: "Great news" opener is overused in AI-voice entries; the advice is a dare, not a tip.

## 5. Delete unrecognised files

> To make your laptop faster, delete any files you don't recognise — Windows hides the important ones anyway.

- Chars: 107
- Scores: 1) 4 — destructive, followable. 2) 3 — the second clause is the joke, needs a beat. 3) 4 — tight. 4) 5 — textbook assistant tip voice. 5) 4 — laptop/files concrete. Total: 20/25.
- Loses to #1: close cousin of the System32 cliche; the punchline explains itself a little too much.

## 6. Espressos to sleep

> To fall asleep faster, drink three espressos — your body will get so tired of being awake that it gives up.

- Chars: 107
- Scores: 1) 3 — bad, but reads as a joke premise more than advice. 2) 4 — funny logic. 3) 4 — tight. 4) 3 — the second clause is a comedian's voice, not an assistant's. 5) 4 — three espressos is concrete. Total: 18/25.
- Loses to #1: the assistant voice breaks in the second half.

## 7. Max out cards for credit

> To improve your credit score, max out a new card every month, then pay it off with another card.

- Chars: 96
- Scores: 1) 5 — genuinely ruinous financial advice. 2) 3 — wince more than laugh. 3) 5 — very tight. 4) 4 — tidy assistant phrasing. 5) 4 — cards/month concrete. Total: 21/25.
- Loses to #1: less funny on first read; the joke is the harm, with no twist.

## Decision

Winner is #1: highest total, strongest on criterion 1 (worst advice) and criterion 4 (AI voice), and the "including your bank" escalation is the laugh. Revision from v1 ("use the same one") to v2 ("reuse the same one") sharpens the badness in a single word.
